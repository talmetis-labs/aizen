# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aizen to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background,
or identity. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask questions before assuming malice.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Keep discussion on-topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments — public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and in
public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer privately.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or
contributions that violate this code, and may ban — temporarily or permanently — any contributor for
behaviors they deem inappropriate.
