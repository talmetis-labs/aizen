## How to agree

You do not sign this document by hand. When You open Your first pull request, an automated check will
post a comment asking You to confirm. To agree, **post a comment on Your pull request with exactly
this sentence:**

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records Your GitHub username and the commit against this Agreement. Once recorded, all Your
future pull requests to this Project are covered automatically and You will not be asked again unless
this Agreement's version changes.

If You are agreeing on behalf of a company, use a company-authorized account and note the company
name in the pull request.
